🥨 Piertoberfest — Oktoberfest on the St. Pete Pier

4th annual Bavarian takeover on the pier, and honestly the most fun thing on the board if you like beer, food, and music: oompah band (Apple Strudel Oktoberfest Band, 2–5 PM), German DJ Keytone, authentic German beers (Paulaner, Radeberger, Hack), schnitzel-and-pretzel food, traditional dancers, and competitions for stein-hoisting, keg throwing, tug-of-war, plus a costume contest. Admission is free; the play is the $25 commemorative stein with two pints pre-purchased online ($41 value). Best stretch: arrive ~4 PM — cool enough, band's encore just wrapped, DJ takes over, sunset over the bay, and you're deep in the party before the drizzle even thinks about showing up.

WhereSt. Pete Pier, Family Park — 740 1st Ave NE 📍
Phonevia stpetepier.org (no event line published)
CostFree entry · $25 stein + 2 pints (pre-purchase recommended)
87–90° at kickoff — grab shade and a pint. 25–41% light-drizzle odds 6–8 PM, gone by 9, when the party's just hitting stride. Pier is open-air: light rain shell, not umbrella.
